01
What holds the room
i.

Chosen

Who is in the room decides everything that follows. Seats are not filled. They are chosen, one by one.

ii.

Early

You meet the artists who will define this generation before the market names its price. Early is the whole advantage.

iii.

Private

What you collect and who you meet stays in the room. No lists, no press, no names shared without your say.

02

The room is small. On purpose.

An invitation-only circle of serious collectors. You meet the artists who'll define this generation before the market does. Not a mailing list. A room worth being in.

The art world has always moved the same way: quietly, early, among the connected. The artists you'll wish you'd found early are showing their work right now, in rooms you're not in. Artifice changes who's connected.
